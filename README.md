# HR-Analytics-Dashboard-Using-Power-BI
- PowerBI HR Aalytics Dashboard featuring employee KPIs, attrition analysis by education, age, job role, salary slab, years at company, and gender, with interactive department slicers&amp; department-wise  employee trends.
# Project Overview:
- Developed an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard using PowerBI to Analyze employee data, understand employee attrition patterns, and identify important workforce insights.
- The dashboard provides key performance indicators(KPIs) and visualizations to explore employee attrition based on education, age, job role, salary slab, years at company, and gender.
- Interactive department slicers allow users to filter the dashboard and compare employee data across  different departments.
# tools & technologies:
-powerBI
-Powerquery
-DAx
#  Key Performance Indicators(KPIs):
The Dashboard includes the followoing KPIs:-
- Employee Count
- Attrition Count
- Average Age
- Average Salary 
- Average Years at company
# Attrition Analysis:
Analyzed employee Attrition across six categories:
# 1) Attrition By Education
- Analyzed employee attrtion across education fields to understand education-wise attrition patterns.
# 2) Attrition By Age
- Compared attrition across different age groups to identify groups with higher employee attrition.
# 3) Attrition By Job Role
- Analyzed employee attrition across different job roles to identify roles experiencing higher attrition.
# 4) Attrition By Years At Company
- Analyzed attrition based on employees years of service to understand  experience-wise attrition patterns.
# 5) Attrition By Salary Slab
- Compared employee attrition across different salary ranges to understand salary-wise attrition patterns.
# 6) Attrition By Gender
- Compared male and female employee attrition rates to understand gender-wise differences.
# Interactive Dashboard Features
1) Department Slicers
- Human Resources
- Research & Development
- Sales
2) Dynamic Filtering
- KPI cards update according to the selected department.
- charts change dynamically based on slicer selections.
- gender filters allow male and female employee comparisons.
- Enables department-wise employee data analysis.
- Provides an interactive dashboard experience.
# Key Results & Insights:-
- Male  employees had a higher attrtion rate(15.5%) than female employees(11.3%).
- Employees aged 26-35 showed the highest attrtion in the HR department.
- Employees in the Up to 5K salary slab showed higher attrtion in the HR department.
- The Human Resources education category had the highest share in the HR education chart.
- Higher attrition was observed during the initial years of employment.
- Interactive slicers helped compare attrtion across departments and genders.
# Attrition Rate - Insights:
- Created a DAX measure to calculate Attrition Rate.
- Analyzed employee attrtion across different job roles, education backgrounds and salary slabs.
- used interactive slicers to explore data dynamically.
- Male employees had a higher attrtion rate(15.5%)than female employees(11.3%).
- The HR department recorded an attrtion rate of 19%.
- Female employees in the sales department showed an attrtion rate of 20.1% with the sales and female filters selected.
- The results show that attrtion rates vary across gender and departments.
- The dashboard helps identify employee groups with higher attrtion.
- main insight: Attrtion Rate comparison helps identify which employee groups are more likely to leave the organization.
